How much does a bucks or hens party cost per person in Australia?

A weekend away usually lands somewhere between a few hundred and over a thousand dollars per guest, depending on the accommodation and activities. The quickest way to know is to plug in real quotes above before you send the invite, so guests can say yes knowing the cost.

Does the bride or groom pay for their own bucks or hens?

Traditionally, no. The guests split the guest of honour's share between them. Choose "Goes free" above and the calculator spreads their share of every cost, including their drinks, across the paying guests.

How do you split drinks if some guests don't drink?

Non-drinkers share the accommodation, activities, food and transport, but not the drinks. The drinks total is split between the paying guests who drink, so nobody pays for a bar tab they didn't use.

How much deposit should the organiser collect?

Collect enough to cover the bookings that need paying upfront, usually the accommodation and activity deposits. Half the per-person cost is a common choice, and it also confirms who's really coming.
