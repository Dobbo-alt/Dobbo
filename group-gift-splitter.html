How much should each person put in for a teacher gift?

For a class collection in Australia, A$5 to A$20 per family is common, and a set amount keeps it fair for everyone. Use "I know what each person gives" to see what that buys once the card is paid for.

How much do you chip in for a work farewell gift?

A$5 to A$20 each is typical for a workmate, often more for a long-serving colleague or a close team. Rounding everyone's share to an easy number makes the collection quicker, and any spare can go on the card as a voucher.

What do you do with leftover money from a group gift?

Most organisers add it to the gift as a voucher, put it toward flowers or the card, or keep it for the next collection. Say which up front so nobody wonders where it went.

What if not everyone pays?

Plan for a few people forgetting. The budget tab shows what you'd have if three people don't pay, so you can choose a gift that still works.
