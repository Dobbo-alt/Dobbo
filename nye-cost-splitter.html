    How do we split NYE costs fairly when friends spend different amounts?

    Log every cost with who paid it and who it was for. Each person's share is added up, compared with what they actually paid, and the calculator works out the fewest bank transfers to square everyone up.

    How do we split the Airbnb if some friends stay fewer nights?

    Set each person's nights, then choose "Accommodation" for the booking. The cost is split per person-night, so someone staying one night of two pays half as much as someone staying both.

    Can we split Uber fares and drinks separately?

    Yes. Add each as its own cost and tick only the people who shared it. Non-drinkers don't pay for the drinks, and only the people in the car pay for the ride.
