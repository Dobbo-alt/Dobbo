How much should a Christmas party cost per person?

It depends on the venue, but the fair way to split it is the same everywhere: share the food across everyone and the drinks across the people drinking. Put in your venue's quote and a realistic bar tab to see the per-head cost before you book.

How do you split a Christmas party bill when some people don't drink?

Split the food and any venue hire evenly, then split the bar tab only between the drinkers. That's what this calculator does, so non-drinkers aren't paying for rounds they didn't have.

What if work is paying for part of it?

Enter the total amount work or the organiser is putting in. It comes off everyone's share equally. If it covers more than someone's share, they pay nothing and the leftover is shown in the note.

How much should you spend on Kris Kringle?

Whatever limit your group agrees on. Each person buys one gift up to the limit, so it's added to your all-in cost, not split.
