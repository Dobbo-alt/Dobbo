What's the difference between Kris Kringle and Secret Santa?

Nothing, really. Australians usually call it Kris Kringle and Americans call it Secret Santa. Everyone draws one name and buys that person a gift up to an agreed limit.

How much should you spend on Kris Kringle?

Most Australian workplaces pick a limit between A$20 and A$50. Choose a figure everyone can afford, and stick to it so nobody feels awkward on the day.

How do you stop people drawing their partner?

Put them on the same line with a plus sign, like "Mia + Tom". The draw never pairs people on the same line, and nobody can draw themselves.
